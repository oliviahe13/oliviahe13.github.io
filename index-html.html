Movie Reviews

Welcome to my simple movie reviews page. Here you can look at my reviews for popular movies for teens and adults, including some of my favorites.

​
​

Movie 1: Zootopia 2 (2025)

​

Director: Byron Howard and Rich Moore ​

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.6

I love this movie for its storyline and action, and the friendship rivalry between Judy and Nick. In my opinion,

the second one is better and has more plot twists and new characters. It has great animation and places based on

animal habitats, and the fact that the animal's personality and actions match the real animal in real life makes the movie much more realistic.

​

Movie 2: Wreck-It Ralph (2012)

​

Director: Rich Moore ​

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.7

This movie is extremely underrated and I love how they bring nostalgic characters to life, such as Sonic The Hedgehog and PacMan.

This movie shows the perspective of the villains and how hard life is for them to do their job and be hated.

It shows how characters come to life when the arcade closes and what happens behind the scenes. 100% a family movie,

this will make you laugh, cry, and wonder about it the next time you go to the arcade.

​

Movie 3: Big Hero Six (2014)

​

Director: Don Hall and Chris Williams ​

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.6

With insane plot twists and heartache for loved ones, Big Hero Six has teamwork, friendship, and betrayal in one.

I love Baymax's simple design and how he develops emotions over time to his friends. There are many different weapon designs fit for each

character and their personality, and all of them are so creative.
