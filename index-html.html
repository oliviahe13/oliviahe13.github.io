Movie 1: Zootopia 2 (2025)

​

Director: Byron Howard and Rich Moore

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.6

I love this movie for its storyline and action, and the friendship rivalry between Judy and Nick. In my opinion,

the second one is better and has more plot twists and new characters. It has great animation and places based on

animal habitats, and the fact that the animal's personality and actions match the real animal in real life makes

the movie much more realistic.

Movie 2: Wreck-It Ralph (2012)

Director: Rich Moore

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.7

This movie is extremely underrated and I love how they bring nostalgic characters to life, such as Sonic The Hedgehog

and PacMan. It shows the perspective of video game villains and how hard life is for them to do their job and still be hated.

Showing the importance of each characters' role, this will make you laugh, cry, and wonder about how the characters survived

their hardships and background stories.

Movie 3: Big Hero Six (2014)

​

Director: Don Hall and Chris Williams

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.6

With insane plot twists and heartache for loved ones, Big Hero Six has teamwork, friendship, and betrayal in one.

I love Baymax's simple and cute design and how he develops emotions over time to his friends. There are many different

weapon designs fit for eachcharacter and their personality, and all of them are so creative. I learned so much about

the science and technology behind all the experiments and gadgets that the heroes used to defeat the villlain.
