gets back, his daughter is already grown up. I also remember that the plot twist at the end was very compelling.

Movie 2: The Dark Knight (2008)

Director: Christopher Nolan

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.8

I 100% remember The Dark Knight, as I have watched this many times with my younger brother Oliver. Batman is fighting the Joker, and because

the Joker keeps killing people to find out who Batman is, people are saying that Batman should reveal himself. The D.A. attorney

Harvey Dent is friends with Bruce Wayne, but doesn’t know his identity. To stop chaos, Harvey reveals he is Batman in front of the public.

Later, the Joker abducts Harvey and his fiance, threatening Batman to only choose one. Bruce tries to save both, but he only reaches Harvey

in time. His fiance dies and half of his face is burned off in the fire, causing him to hate Batman. In the end, Harvey commits suicide.

If you love loud explosions, fast-action, and a retelling of Batman, you should watch this movie.

Movie 3: Tenet (2020)

Director: Christopher Nolan

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.9

Tenet is so confusing to watch, I had to rewatch it four times in my entire life and I still don’t get it completely. The plot is based on

the name: TENET, which is a palindrome that is spelled the same forwards and backwards. The same is with the story: anyone who goes through

this machine, suddenly everything is backwards. Gunshot holes are already in the wall, heat feels cold, and damage from a destruction is

already at the scene. The main character, who doesn’t have a name, is trying to save this woman from her abusive husband who wants to destroy

the whole world just because he is going to die of cancer. If you want to lose sleep over a plot so deep and confusing, watch Tenet.
