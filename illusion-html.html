Movie 1: The Matrix (1999)

Director: Lilly and Lana Wachowski

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.8

I watched this movie with my dad, and it was utterly mind-blowing. In this era, everyone’s lives are all fake and they

are basically living in a virtual reality in which an evil AI controls. Humanity lives in these pods that give the AI access

to all their emotions, thoughts, memories, and actions, creating a simulation for them to live in. There are only a few people still alive,

and they are trying to stop the AI from controlling them, but there is an inside man in their team. If you like movies that show the darker

side of reality with fast action, this movie is for you.

Movie 2: The Truman Show (1998)

Director: Peter Weir

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.7

This show is truly depressing to watch. While watching this, I felt so bad for Truman, and constantly considered how lucky

I was to live a life of pure reality. Truman’s whole life is for a TV show, with millions of people watching live. His birth, his best friend,

his wife are all actors that he believes are genuine. Living in the largest stage set ever created, Truman starts finding clues that reality isn’t

what it seems, especially when he tries to leave the city to find the love of his life.

Movie 3: Now You See Me (2013)

Director: Louis Leterrier

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.8

This is one of my favourite movies of all time, with teamwork, magic, and plot twists. The story of 4 magicians working together to

complete a heist, this movie shows how easy it is to fool even the best networks and people. Tricking human eyes and robbing a bank, the

magicians finally realize what it was all for.
